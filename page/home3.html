ABOUT OUR

ORGANISATION

DDelivery started as a small, one-van operation in the back alleys of Brooklyn, but within three years it had grown into the most unexpectedly reliable logistics network in the tri-state area. What set DDelivery apart wasn't speed or flashy tracking apps—though they had both—it was the bizarrely specific attention to detail. Every driver, for instance, carried a small pouch of spare buttons, because the founder once missed an important package delivery when a client's coat button popped off right as they were signing for a parcel. That same founder, a former jazz drummer named Elena Vasquez, insisted that all delivery vans be painted a shade she called "submarine yellow," and that every package weighing under two pounds be greeted with a handwritten "thumbs up" drawn on the tape. Customers began requesting the same drivers, tipping them in homemade jam and handwritten notes, and soon DDelivery had a waitlist for their services. They don't deliver everywhere, and they never will—Elena believes that growth for growth's sake ruins the rhythm—but if you live within their odd, amoeba-shaped delivery zone, you know that when the yellow van pulls up, whatever arrives will arrive with the quiet dignity of something that was cared for along the way.
